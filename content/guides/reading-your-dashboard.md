Every character in Hard Will has a dashboard with four tabs. Here is what each one is for.

## Overview

Your character at a glance. The header shows level, class, world rank (for example *Solis #12,675*) and EXP, synced from the Nexon rankings. The cards show:

- **Level**, with the days until your next level at your current pace
- **Items tracked** and how many have legendary potential
- **Star force**, **flame score** and **potential %** for your main stat
- **Meso** from your last snapshot
- **Next HEXA upgrade**, with the fragments needed and when you will have them

## Equipment

Your gear laid out like the in-game equipment window. Each slot shows its star force. Click a slot to see the item, or double-click to open its tooltip and history.

Use **equipment presets** (1 to 3) to keep separate sets, for example a bossing set and a leveling set.

## History

A timeline of every snapshot and recorded item. Use it to see how far you have come since the start of an event, or when an item last changed.

## Tools

Calculators that use your character's real data and the GMS v.271 formulas:

- **Star force**: expected meso cost and booms from one star to another, with Safeguard, MVP discounts, events and Enhancement Mode.
- **Leveling**: EXP left to your next level for levels 260 to 299.
- **Symbols**: Arcane and Sacred symbols and meso needed to max them, with daily income.
- **Potential**: how close your main-stat % is to common goals, such as 33% on a Legendary item.

## Trackers

- **Boss Tracker**: weekly and daily bosses per character.
- **Daily Tracker**: symbol dailies and other daily content, with the reset countdown.
- **HEXA & Liberation**: fragment income, Sol Erda and Genesis or Destiny liberation progress.

## Game HUD

Press **Game HUD** to shrink Hard Will to a capsule on top of MapleStory. From there you can capture, and switch between Bosses and Dailies to tick things off without alt-tabbing. Press **Open app** to return.

## Upgrade ideas

Hard Will looks across your recorded gear and points out inconsistencies and cheap wins, such as an item that is far behind the rest of your set.
