Item recording is the fastest way to get your whole equipment into Hard Will. Instead of typing stats, you hover items in game and Hard Will reads each tooltip.

## Before you start

- Take a [character snapshot](/guides/getting-started) first so Hard Will knows which character the gear belongs to.
- Open your **Equipment** window in MapleStory (default key: `E`).

## Record

1. Press **Start recording**. Hard Will shrinks to a floating **REC** chip in the top-right corner of your screen.
2. Switch to MapleStory and hover each item. Hold still for about **one second** per item so the full tooltip is on screen.
3. Press stop on the REC chip to come back to Hard Will.

## What gets read

For every item tooltip, Hard Will reads:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Star force | ★22 |
| Potential tier and lines | Legendary · STR +13% · STR +10% · All Stat +7% |
| Bonus potential | Unique · ATT +12 |
| Flames (bonus stats) | STR +45, ATT +6 |
| Scroll / enhancement stats | ATT +40 |

## Understand the summary

After you stop, the summary counts every hover:

- **Saved**: a new item or a changed item was recorded.
- **Duplicates**: an item you already recorded, unchanged.
- **Not items**: no item tooltip was visible.
- **Unclear**: the tooltip could not be read reliably, so it was not saved. Hover that item again.
- **Errors**: something went wrong; details are in the error log.

## Tips

- Hover the same item again any time it changes, for example after a star force session or flame reroll. Its **History** keeps every version.
- In the **Equipment** tab, drag an item onto another slot to match your in-game window. Double-click a slot to see the tooltip and its history.
